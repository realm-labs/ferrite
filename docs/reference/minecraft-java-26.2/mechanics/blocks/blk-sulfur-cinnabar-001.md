Raw Sulfur registers yellow map color, Base Drum, required-correct-tool drops,
strength `1.5/6` and `SoundType.SULFUR`. Polished, brick and chiseled forms use
`ofLegacyCopy(SULFUR)` and therefore retain that entire profile. Raw Cinnabar
has the same values with red map color and `SoundType.CINNABAR`; its three
processed forms legacy-copy it.

Both sound types use volume/pitch `1/1`. Sulfur break/step/place/hit/fall
sound-event protocol IDs are `1912/1913/1914/1915/1916`; Cinnabar uses
`1926/1927/1928/1929/1930`.

Every block is an ordinary full cube with default friction `0.6`, speed/jump
factors `1`, light `0`, opaque occlusion, ordinary survival and piston reaction
`NORMAL`. None overrides placement, use, update, tick, entity contact,
redstone, comparator, fluid or clone behavior. All eight are directly
`mineable/pickaxe`; none belongs to a higher-tier requirement tag. Any
Pickaxe is therefore a correct harvest tool, while a non-Pickaxe fails the
required-tool harvest gate.

Every item is a common stack-64 `BlockItem` with no custom component beyond
its item model/name and generic defaults. All eight items directly belong to
`sulfur_cube_archetype/slow_bouncy`.

**Transition and ordering:**

Placement, breaking, recipe execution, item-equipment dispatch and generation
enter their generic owners before consulting the identity joins below. The
eight registered blocks add no local state transition; raw-only world
selectors are evaluated independently of the copied physical profiles.

**Breaking and loot:**

Each block has a distinct block loot table with one roll and one exact
count-one self item, guarded only by `survives_explosion`; each random sequence
is `minecraft:blocks/<identity>`. No Silk Touch, Fortune, state or alternate
drop branch exists.

The registration-level correct-tool flag still controls player harvest before
loot is emitted: any Pickaxe can obtain the self drop, while other tools or
hands do not. Explosion evaluation is governed by the table's independent
survival condition.

No identity has a `FireBlock` encouragement/flammability entry, lava-ignition
property, vanilla fuel time or Composter chance.

**Crafting and cutting graph:**

The two materials have isomorphic ordinary masonry graphs:

- Four raw blocks in a 2x2 square produce four Polished blocks.
- Four Polished blocks in a 2x2 square produce four Bricks.
- Two raw-material Slabs in one column produce one Chiseled block.
- Each raw, Polished and Brick identity has the standard shaped Stairs
  (`6 -> 4`), Slab (`3 -> 6`) and Wall (`6 -> 6`) recipe.

Sulfur additionally has a 2x2 four-Sulfur-Spike recipe yielding one Sulfur and
a shapeless nine-Sulfur recipe yielding one Potent Sulfur. Cinnabar has no
equivalent extra conversion.

There are exactly 44 stonecutting records, 22 per material. For either
material:

- One raw block cuts to one Chiseled, Polished, Bricks, raw Stairs/Wall,
  Polished Stairs/Wall or Brick Stairs/Wall, and to two of each raw, Polished
  or Brick Slab: 12 outputs.
- One Polished block cuts to one Bricks, Polished Stairs/Wall or Brick
  Stairs/Wall, and two Polished or Brick Slabs: seven outputs.
- One Bricks block cuts to one Brick Stairs/Wall or two Brick Slabs: three
  outputs.

Together with 25 shaped and one shapeless record, the locked graph totals 70
recipes. Seventy same-named recipe advancements mirror those records. Each
uses one OR requirement: already knowing the recipe or possessing the source
ingredient appropriate to that recipe; the reward unlocks exactly that one
recipe. Generic crafting and stonecutter owners retain match, consumption,
output-slot and knowledge mechanics.

`BlockFamilies` encodes raw -> wall/stairs/slab/chiseled/polished, Polished ->
wall/stairs/slab/bricks, and Bricks -> wall/stairs/slab for both materials,
with stonecutter generation enabled for all three family tiers. The locked
JSON above is authoritative at runtime.

**Sulfur Cube consumer:**

An adult Sulfur Cube with an empty BODY slot may absorb one of any of the eight
items through the generic swallowable/`slow_bouncy` path. Ground pickup also
requires a live item entity, no pickup delay and pickup timer at most zero; it
splits exactly one, marks the body stack as a guaranteed drop and plays the
absorb sound. Direct equipment rejects babies and an identical existing item,
may drop the previous body stack server-side, then installs a count-one copy.

`slow_bouncy` applies:

- knockback-resistance and explosion-knockback-resistance `add_value`
  modifiers `0.4000000059604645`;
- bounciness `add_value` `0.6000000238418579`;
- friction total multiplier `-0.699999988079071`;
- air-drag total multiplier `-0.9499999992549419`;
- horizontal/vertical knockback power `0.4125/0.24`;
- slow-bouncy hit/push sounds, push cooldown `0.5` and impulse threshold
  `0.05`.

The archetype is selected by the live item tag. Removing an identity from that
tag stops future matching without changing existing block or item registry
state.

**Raw-material world joins:**

Only raw Sulfur and raw Cinnabar directly join
`overworld_carver_replaceables`, `sculk_replaceable` and
`sulfur_spike_replaceable_blocks`. They can therefore be carved, replaced by
the generic sculk conversion path and replaced while Sulfur speleothems grow.
The spike-replaceable tag contains exactly those two blocks.

The configured Glow Lichen multiface feature includes both raw states in its
explicit support list. Processed forms are absent from all four selectors.

### Sulfur-Caves surface material

Each of the five locked Overworld-derived noise settings—Overworld, Large
Biomes, Amplified, Caves and Floating Islands—contains the same ordered
Sulfur-Caves material sequence in three surface-rule contexts. When the biome
is exact `sulfur_caves`, three 3D `sulfur_cave_gradient` tests run in order:

1. `[-0.4000000059604645, -0.10000000149011612]` -> Cinnabar;
2. `[0, 0.4000000059604645]` -> Sulfur;
3. `[0.4000000059604645, Double.MAX_VALUE]` -> Cinnabar;
4. otherwise -> Stone.

Generic surface-rule ordering owns threshold equality; in particular the
earlier Sulfur result wins at the shared `0.4000000059604645` endpoint. The
biome's underground climate point and noise-router execution remain
`WGEN-PIPELINE-001`.

### Other Sulfur features

`sulfur_pool` uses Sulfur as a lake barrier. Its placed wrapper makes 256
in-square, uniform-height attempts, requires an initial solid position, scans
up at most 32 to air, offsets down one, then requires exact Sulfur before the
biome gate. Its sequence later places wet Potent Sulfur on scanned water
surfaces; that other block retains separate ownership.

The simple Sulfur Spike selector chooses uniformly between downward and upward
environment scans of at most 12, then uses Sulfur as the speleothem base.
The cluster uses Sulfur base, uniform radius `2..8`, height `1..4`, layer
thickness `2..4`, density `0.3..<0.7`, edge chance `0.1`, search range `12`,
height deviation `3`, bias distances `8/3`, maximum opposing-height
difference `1` and wetness `0`. Their placed wrappers and generic
speleothem/lake/root/template algorithms remain `WGEN-PIPELINE-001`.

Sulfur Caves schedules rooted spring and pool at underground-decoration step,
spikes and clusters at underground-decoration step seven, and Glow Lichen
later. Raw terrain may therefore be consumed or covered by later features and
carvers according to the generic chunk pipeline.

**Client projection:**

Each identity has one empty blockstate variant selecting its same-named block
model. Every model is `block/cube_all` with one same-named texture, and every
item definition selects that block model directly. There is no tint,
multipart state, random variant, animation, special renderer or independent
flat item texture.

Building Blocks presents each material as raw, Stairs, Slab, Wall, Chiseled,
Polished, Polished Stairs/Slab/Wall, Bricks, Brick Stairs/Slab/Wall, with the
Cinnabar group immediately before Sulfur. Raw Cinnabar and Sulfur also appear
in Ingredients before Sulfur Spike and Potent Sulfur.

registered single-state schemas.
