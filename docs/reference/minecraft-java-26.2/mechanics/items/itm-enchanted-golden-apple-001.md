**Authoritative state:**

`minecraft:enchanted_golden_apple` is raw item ID `1015`. It uses the plain-item registration
path, is rare, nondamageable and has maximum stack `64`. Its prototype supplies:

- food nutrition `4`, saturation `9.6` and `can_always_eat=true`;
- the otherwise-default `1.6`-second (`32`-tick) eat consumable;
- one apply-effects consumer containing fresh Regeneration amplifier `1` for `400` ticks,
  Resistance amplifier `0` for `6000` ticks, Fire Resistance amplifier `0` for `6000` ticks and
  Absorption amplifier `3` for `2400` ticks, in that order;
- `minecraft:rarity=rare`; and
- `minecraft:enchantment_glint_override=true` alongside an empty enchantments component.

All four effects use default nonambient/particle behavior and explicitly show their icons. The
remaining components are the common empty modifiers/lore, item-break sound, translated name,
direct item-model key, repair cost, swing animation, tooltip display and use effects. There is no
cooldown, use remainder, tool, equipment or repairable state.

Its direct tags are `#minecraft:horse_food`, `#minecraft:horse_tempt_items` and
`#minecraft:piglin_loved`. Zombie-Villager curing does not use any of those tags or compare food
components: it tests exact plain Golden Apple identity, excluding this item.

**Transition and ordering:**

Player consumption and ordered effects:

In-air player use enters the consumable path because the registered food is always edible; an
ordinary survival player can start at food level `20`. An admitted use starts the default eat
animation with `CONSUME`. Block and entity clicks retain their earlier interaction dispatch before
an unconsumed result can reach the edible-item fallback.

Removing only the food component leaves the consumable and all four effects intact. `canConsume`
then has no food gate, use remains admitted at full hunger, and completion applies no nutrition or
saturation. Removing only the consumable makes plain in-air use pass and prevents food, effects
and shrink. A patched food component supplies its live nutrition, saturation and always-edible
flag while the unchanged consumable retains the four effect offers.

Interruption, release or live-hand/component replacement before completion commits no statistic,
criterion, nutrition, effect, game event or shrink. At successful server completion, generic
active-use ordering:

1. emits the configured final eat effects;
2. awards the item-used statistic and triggers `consume_item` against the live pre-shrink stack;
3. runs the food listener, clamping hunger after `+4` and saturation after `+9.6`;
4. runs the single apply-effects consumer, drawing one entity RNG float and entering on the
   omitted probability default `1.0`;
5. offers fresh Regeneration `400@1`, Resistance `6000@0`, Fire Resistance `6000@0` and
   Absorption `2400@3` in that order;
6. emits the `EAT` game event and shrinks one unless the user has infinite materials.

Probability one still spends its one float before the strict comparison; the four effects do not
draw independently. Immunity and existing-effect strength, duration, hidden chains and callbacks
can independently reject or merge an offer under `ENT-EFFECT-001` without preventing later offers
or rolling back nutrition, event or consumption. There is no remainder. Infinite-material use
retains the Apple while still committing the admitted transaction.

Enchanted Golden Apple is one of the `40` independent AND requirements in
`husbandry/balanced_diet`. Because `consume_item` precedes food and effects, successful completion
advances its requirement from the pre-shrink stack even when one or more effect offers are
rejected. The advancement display itself uses an ordinary Apple icon; this item is a criterion,
not the icon.

Mojang Banner Pattern crafting and recipe knowledge:

No locked recipe creates an Enchanted Golden Apple. Its sole recipe occurrence is as an ingredient
in the shapeless `mojang_banner_pattern` recipe. One Paper plus one Enchanted Golden Apple in any
otherwise-empty crafting grid produces one default `minecraft:mojang_banner_pattern`. Ingredient
positions are irrelevant; any extra item rejects the match. Taking the result consumes both inputs
through ordinary crafting, leaves no remainder and does not execute the Apple's food effects.

The recipe-unlock advancement has parent `recipes/root` and one OR requirement: either the player
possesses an Enchanted Golden Apple or the exact recipe is already unlocked. Completion awards
that recipe to the recipe book. Recipe result construction, input removal, knowledge/highlight
state and the result's later Loom behavior remain with `ITM-RECIPE-001`, `ITM-CRAFT-001`,
`ITM-ADVANCEMENT-001` and `ITM-LOOM-001`.

Base structure and dungeon loot:

Every listed target entry is unconditional. Unless noted, it has no function and emits one default
Apple when selected. Fractions are per pool roll, before later container insertion:

- Abandoned Mineshaft pool `0` rolls once and selects the Apple at weight `1/71`. Pools `1` and
  `2` later roll `2..4` and exactly `3` times.
- Ancient City pool `0` rolls `5..10` times and selects it at weight `1/84`; `set_count` then
  draws `1..2`. Pool `1` later rolls once.
- Bastion Treasure pool `0` rolls exactly `3` times and selects it at weight `2/112=1/56`;
  explicit `set_count=1` is deterministic. Later pools roll `3..4`, `1` and `1`.
- Desert Pyramid pool `0` rolls `2..4` times and selects it at weight `2/247`. Later pools roll
  `4` and `1`.
- Ruined Portal pool `0` rolls `4..8` times and selects it at weight `1/398`. Pool `1` later rolls
  once.
- Simple Dungeon pool `0` rolls `1..3` times and selects it at weight `2/144=1/72`. Later pools
  roll `1..4` and `3`.
- Woodland Mansion pool `0` rolls `1..3` times and selects it at weight `2/107`. Later pools roll
  `1..4`, `3` and `1`.

Repeated rolls select with replacement, so one evaluation can emit multiple stacks. The exact
roll, entry, count and later-pool draws advance each table's named random sequence
`minecraft:chests/<table>` under `ITM-LOOT-001`; merging/insertion can alter final slots without
changing the generated sequence.

Ominous Trial/Vault loot:

The ordinary Trial reward root and its leaves contain no Enchanted Golden Apple. The ominous root
uses its own `minecraft:chests/trial_chambers/reward_ominous` sequence:

1. pool `0` rolls once between ominous-rare weight `8/10` and ominous-common weight `2/10`;
2. pool `1` rolls ominous-common `1..3` times;
3. pool `2` makes one `random_chance=0.75` draw and, on success, invokes
   `reward_ominous_unique`.

The unique leaf has its separate
`minecraft:chests/trial_chambers/reward_ominous_unique` sequence, rolls once and selects the
Apple at weight `3/10`, count one, with no function. Its marginal selection probability per
ominous-root evaluation is therefore `(3/4)*(3/10)=9/40`; the earlier rare/common work still
occurs and advances only its owning root or nested sequences.

Trade-rebalance replacements:

Enabling the built-in trade-rebalance pack replaces whole table records rather than patching
entries. All three replacements preserve the base `random_sequence` name:

- Abandoned Mineshaft keeps the target pool exactly `1/71`, count one, and preserves the next
  `2..4`-roll and `3`-roll pools. It adds a fourth one-roll pool selecting empty with probability
  `4/5` or an Efficiency-enchanted Book at `1/5` after all target work.
- Ancient City keeps the target pool at `1/84` per each of `5..10` rolls and count `1..2`; replacing
  Leather by same-weight Saddle does not change its denominator. Its later one-roll pool changes
  from empty `75`, Ward template `4`, Silence template `1` to empty `71`, Mending Book `4`, Ward
  `4`, Silence `1`.
- Desert Pyramid keeps target weight `2` but changes the first-pool denominator from `247` to
  `237`, hence `2/237` per each of `2..4` rolls. Its later pools remain `4` and `1` rolls, with the
  final pool selecting empty `4`, Dune template `1` or Unbreaking Book `2`.

Pack selection happens before evaluation. An evaluation cannot combine base and replacement
pools, and pack reload changes only future table resolution.

Zombie-Villager non-cure boundary:

`ZombieVillager.mobInteract` compares the held stack only with `Items.GOLDEN_APPLE`. An Enchanted
Golden Apple therefore skips the cure specialization even when the target has Weakness or is
already converting. The delegated plain Zombie/Mob interaction has no action for this plain item,
returns `PASS`, emits no entity-interact event, consumes nothing, changes no conversion starter or
timer and offers no Strength/event `16`.

Neither matching food/effect components nor membership in the three tags changes that exclusion.
A player must perform a separate in-air use to eat this item; an entity-click packet does not
become the edible-item fallback. The full plain-Golden-Apple cure transaction remains
`ITM-GOLDEN-APPLE-001`.

Ordinary Horse, Donkey and Mule feeding:

Unridden Horse and Abstract-Chested-Horse interaction consult live `horse_food` before the shared
hard-coded feed table, so Enchanted Golden Apple reaches ordinary Horse, Donkey and Mule unless an
earlier adult-tamed secondary-use inventory branch wins. Llama, Camel and Zombie Horse use other
food selectors; an ordinary unmounted Skeleton Horse bypasses the specialized feed-table check.

Both Golden Apple identities share the same direct feed-table row: heal `10`, baby growth `240`
seconds and temper increment `10`. On the server, the table first puts a tamed age-zero equine not
already in love into `600`-tick love with the player as cause. It then, in order:

1. heals `10` when health is below maximum;
2. if age-unlocked and baby, emits the happy-particle offer and ages up `240` seconds;
3. if temper is below maximum and either an earlier effect succeeded or the equine is untamed,
   adds `10` through the clamped temper modifier.

Any success opens the synchronized mouth, plays the subtype eating sound unless silent at volume
`1` and pitch `1+(U1-U2)*0.2`, emits `EAT`, consumes one through the player-ability-aware rule and
returns server success. The registered player food and four effects are not invoked.

A tamed full-health adult not already in love therefore consumes solely for love. An untamed adult
never receives love but can consume for healing or temper. A full-health maximum-temper untamed
adult and a full-health already-loving tamed adult return `PASS`. Babies do not enter love but can
heal, grow and raise temper. Horse and Donkey can later use eligible love state to parent; Mule can
retain love but cannot mate.

Vehicle dispatch and equine temptation:

Horse and Abstract-Chested-Horse delegate vehicle interaction to generic `Animal.mobInteract`
before the specialized feed table. Live `horse_food` membership then consumes one for `600`-tick
adult love or advances an age-unlocked baby by
`floor((remainingBabyTicks/20)*0.1)` seconds. It does not heal, alter temper, open the mouth, play
the equine eating sound, emit specialized `EAT`, or apply player-food effects. Already-loving
adults and age-locked babies consume nothing.

A tamed Skeleton Horse vehicle also delegates to the generic path, so an adult can enter unusable
love while unmounted adults and age-locked babies gain no Apple consequence. Zombie Horse, Llama
and Camel selectors reject direct horse-tag membership. Unnatural untamed vehicle fixtures can
receive generic love because `Animal` tests age/love rather than tame state.

Normal Horse, Donkey and Mule goals separately test `horse_tempt_items`. The nearest player within
the live Tempt Range attribute (baseline `10`) holding Enchanted Golden Apple in either hand can
own the non-line-of-sight goal. It looks at that player and navigates at speed `1.25` until within
`2.5` blocks, cannot be scared by player movement and receives the reduced
`ceil(100/2)=50`-tick cooldown after stopping. Skeleton/Zombie Horse replace this goal set, Llama
uses `llama_tempt_items`, and Camel uses its brain.

Piglin-loved pickup and admiration:

Subject to generic baby-ignore, repellent, attack/admirer, reachability and inventory gates, a
Piglin can want an Enchanted-Golden-Apple item entity. Pickup removes exactly one non-nugget Apple,
leaves the rest in the entity, erases `TIME_TRYING_TO_REACH_ADMIRE_ITEM`, moves the Apple to the
off hand and sets `ADMIRING_ITEM` for `119` ticks. Moving it first drops any previous offhand
stack.

When holding ends, an adult generates no barter loot because exact currency remains Gold Ingot.
It first attempts equipment replacement and otherwise stores the Apple stack, throwing overflow
toward a generic random position. Baby finalization retains its separate policy. A player holding
Enchanted Golden Apple also satisfies `isPlayerHoldingLovedItem`, affecting sensor/look/
nearest-wanted-player and jealous-sound decisions without transfer. Remaining Piglin activity,
memories, navigation, inventory, sound and combat behavior remains `MOB-AI-001`.

**Persistence and reload boundary:**

Stacks persist and synchronize identity, count and arbitrary ordinary component patches. They
store no active-use progress, hunger, recipe knowledge, chest context, table cursor, animal state
or Piglin memory; those belong to their player, world, entity and machine owners.

Loot reload changes future unresolved evaluations, including which base or trade-rebalance
replacement record is active. Recipe/advancement reload changes future matching and listeners;
tag reload changes future equine/Piglin admission and temptation. None changes the exact-identity
Zombie-Villager exclusion. Resource reload independently controls language and model. Completed
eating, crafting, loot, feeding and pickup transactions are not replayed.

**Client and wire projection:**

Generic item-stack encoding projects raw item ID `1015` plus the stack's component patch. The
default rare hover name uses locked English text `Enchanted Golden Apple` with rare/aqua styling
and no subtype tooltip. Its true glint override makes the default stack foil despite empty
enchantments. An explicit patched false value suppresses foil; removing the override delegates to
the generic item/enchantment result.

The direct item definition selects generated model
`minecraft:item/enchanted_golden_apple`; that model deliberately uses
`minecraft:item/golden_apple` as its sole texture layer. No separate
`enchanted_golden_apple.png` exists; the glint is a render layer, not a second item texture.

It appears exactly once in Food & Drinks, ordered Apple, Golden Apple, Enchanted Golden Apple,
Melon Slice. It supplies no tab or advancement icon. Eating projects the four ordinary effect
states through existing effect packet owners. The item adds no packet layout or numeric mapping.

**Boundary cases and quirks:**
