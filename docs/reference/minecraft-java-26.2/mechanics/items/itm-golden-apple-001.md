**Authoritative state:**

`minecraft:golden_apple` is raw item ID `1014`. It is common, nondamageable and has maximum stack
`64`. The plain-item registration supplies:

- food nutrition `4`, saturation `9.6` and `can_always_eat=true`;
- the otherwise-default `1.6`-second (`32`-tick) eat consumable;
- one apply-effects consumer containing fresh Regeneration amplifier `1` for `100` ticks followed
  by Absorption amplifier `0` for `2400` ticks. Both use default nonambient/particle behavior and
  explicitly show their icons.

The remaining registered components are the common empty modifiers/enchantments/lore, item-break
sound, translated name, direct item-model key, repair cost, swing animation, tooltip display and
use effects. There is no cooldown, use remainder, tool, equipment, repairable, rarity override or
identity-specific glint state.

Its direct tags are `#minecraft:horse_food`, `#minecraft:horse_tempt_items` and
`#minecraft:piglin_loved`. Zombie-Villager curing instead tests exact Golden Apple identity:
neither those tags nor the food/consumable components admit that branch.

**Transition and ordering:**

Player consumption and ordered effects:

In-air player use enters the consumable path because the registered food is always edible; an
ordinary survival player can therefore start at food level `20`. An admitted use starts the
default eat animation with `CONSUME`. Block and entity clicks retain their earlier interaction
dispatch before an unconsumed result can reach the edible-item fallback.

Removing only the food component leaves the consumable and both effects intact. `canConsume` then
has no food gate, use remains admitted at full hunger, and completion applies no nutrition or
saturation. Removing only the consumable makes plain in-air use pass and prevents food, effects
and shrink. A patched food component supplies its live nutrition, saturation and always-edible
flag while the unchanged consumable retains the two effect offers.

Interruption, release or live-hand/component replacement before completion commits no statistic,
criterion, nutrition, effect, game event or shrink. At successful server completion, generic
active-use ordering:

1. emits the configured final eat effects;
2. awards the item-used statistic and triggers `consume_item` against the live pre-shrink stack;
3. runs the food listener, clamping hunger after `+4` and saturation after `+9.6`;
4. runs the single apply-effects consumer, drawing one entity RNG float and entering on the
   omitted probability default `1.0`;
5. offers fresh Regeneration `100@1` and Absorption `2400@0` in that order;
6. emits the `EAT` game event and shrinks one unless the user has infinite materials.

Probability one still spends its one float before the strict comparison; the two effects do not
draw independently. Immunity and existing-effect strength, duration, hidden chains and callbacks
can independently reject or merge either offer under `ENT-EFFECT-001` without preventing the
later offer or rolling back nutrition, event or consumption. There is no remainder.
Infinite-material use retains the apple while still committing the admitted transaction.

Golden Apple is one of the `40` independent AND requirements in
`husbandry/balanced_diet`. Because `consume_item` precedes food and effects, successful completion
advances its requirement from the pre-shrink stack even if either effect offer is rejected.

Crafting and recipe knowledge:

The shaped recipe requires a full `3x3` grid: eight Gold Ingots surround one Apple and produce one
default Golden Apple. All nine inputs are consumed on take; neither input has a crafting remainder,
and arbitrary input components do not propagate to the output.

The no-display `recipes/root` child has one OR requirement containing Gold-Ingot possession and
exact `golden_apple` recipe unlock. Either criterion awards only this recipe. Possessing an Apple
or Golden Apple does not satisfy the inventory criterion.

Trade-rebalance replacements:

Enabling the built-in `trade_rebalance` pack replaces, rather than appends to, the entire
Mineshaft and Desert-Pyramid records while retaining their original named sequence.

The replacement Mineshaft first pool is unchanged, so Golden Apple remains `20/71`; its existing
later pools remain `2..4` and `3` rolls, followed by a new one-roll Empty/Efficiency-Book pool
with total weight `5`. The replacement Pyramid still makes `2..4` first-pool rolls but changes
other entries enough to reduce total weight from `247` to `237`; Golden Apple remains weight
`20`, hence `20/237` per roll. Its later pools remain four rolls and one roll. Pack enablement
therefore changes only future unresolved evaluations and does not layer both variants or rewrite
already generated contents.

Trial Chambers reward acquisition:

The ordinary root `chests/trial_chambers/reward` evaluates two non-apple pools first: one
rare/common nested choice and `1..3` common-table rolls. Its third pool then tests random chance
`0.25`; on success `reward_unique` makes one selection from total weight `12`, where Golden Apple
has weight `4`. The conditional chance is `1/3`, and the root marginal is
`1/4 * 1/3 = 1/12`.

The ominous root `chests/trial_chambers/reward_ominous` first selects the ominous-rare table at
weight `8/10` or ominous-common at `2/10`. `reward_ominous_rare` makes one selection from total
weight `29`; Golden Apple has weight `3`. Its root marginal is
`4/5 * 3/29 = 12/145`. The root then evaluates `1..3` ominous-common rolls and a
`0.75`-conditioned ominous-unique roll, neither of which emits Golden Apple.

Root and nested records use their own named sequences. A selected Apple has count one and no
further count draw. Vault admission, key use, ejection, insertion and client projection remain
with `BLK-VAULT-001`, `WGEN-JIGSAW-TRIAL-CHAMBERS-001`, `ITM-LOOT-001` and their protocol owners.

Zombie-Villager cure initiation:

Entity interaction with any component-patched Golden Apple first reads the target Zombie
Villager's live Weakness effect. If Weakness is absent, the specialized branch returns `CONSUME`
without shrinking or starting item use; targeting that entity therefore suppresses the edible
fallback for that interaction. If Weakness is present, it consumes one Apple through the
player-ability-aware rule before the side test. On the authoritative server it then:

1. stores the player's UUID as conversion starter;
2. draws `nextInt(2401) + 3600`, uniformly selecting an inclusive `3600..6000`-tick timer;
3. sets synchronized converting state true and stores that timer;
4. removes Weakness;
5. offers Strength for exactly the initial timer with amplifier
   `min(difficultyId - 1, 0)`: `-1` in Peaceful and `0` in Easy, Normal or Hard;
6. broadcasts entity event `16`, then returns `SUCCESS_SERVER`.

The event plays `entity.zombie_villager.cure` locally unless the entity is silent, at volume `1`
and pitch `U1 + 0.7*U2 + 0.3`, consuming two client entity-RNG floats. The converting metadata is
independently trackable. The Golden Apple's food and apply-effects components never run on this
path, so the target receives neither player nutrition nor the Apple's Regeneration/Absorption.

There is deliberately no `!isConverting` admission test. Because initiation removes Weakness,
ordinary repeated use is blocked; if Weakness is reapplied to a converting Zombie Villager,
another Golden Apple is consumed and overwrites the starter UUID and remaining time, reapplies
Strength through ordinary effect merging and rebroadcasts event `16`.

Conversion countdown and acceleration:

On every loaded, alive, server-side converting tick, progress begins at `1` and consumes one
entity RNG float. Unless that float is strictly below `0.01`, no block scan occurs. On a hit, the
code traverses an eight-by-eight-by-eight box in X/Y/Z nesting, from `(int)coordinate - 4`
inclusive to `(int)coordinate + 4` exclusive on each axis. Java integer conversion truncates
toward zero.

Only exact Iron Bars or any `BedBlock` are special. The scan stops after encountering `14` such
blocks, regardless of how many accelerate. Each encountered special block consumes one float and
adds one progress only when that float is strictly below `0.3`. A tick therefore decrements the
timer by `1..15`; ordinary blocks consume no per-cell random value. The first tick whose
post-decrement timer is at most zero begins conversion immediately, before that Zombie
Villager's delegated Zombie tick.

While converting, the Zombie Villager's distance-despawn override refuses removal. Death/removal
still prevents later conversion, and unloaded entities perform no countdown or wall-clock
catch-up.

Conversion completion:

Completion requests one `minecraft:villager` using spawn reason `CONVERSION` and single-entity
conversion parameters with automatic equipment and pickup-state preservation both false. If
creation fails, no replacement is committed and the still-converting source retries on a later
tick. The ordinary single-conversion transaction gives the new Villager a new UUID and transfers
position, velocity, vehicle, first passenger, leash, fall/ground state, team, absorption, active
effects, baby state, left-handed/no-AI/persistence flags, visible/silent/fire/invulnerable/gravity
state, portal cooldown, entity tags, custom name and custom data as specified by
`ENT-LIFECYCLE-001`.

The Zombie-Villager callback then:

1. handles every nonempty equipment slot. An item carrying
   `PREVENT_ARMOR_CHANGE` is retained into the matching Villager slot; other equipment is dropped
   only when its slot has preserved drop chance and is otherwise discarded with the old entity;
2. transfers finalized Villager Data and Villager Data, optional gossips, a copy of optional
   offers, and Villager XP;
3. runs Villager finalization with spawn reason `CONVERSION` and refreshes its brain;
4. if the stored UUID resolves to a live `ServerPlayer` in this same `ServerLevel`, triggers
   `cured_zombie_villager` with the old Zombie and new Villager, then emits the
   `ZOMBIE_VILLAGER_CURED` reputation event from that player to the Villager;
5. offers the new Villager Nausea `200@0`;
6. unless the old entity is silent, emits level event `1027` at its block position.

The callback runs before the new Villager is added. The generic conversion then adds it and
discards the old Zombie Villager. Active effects are copied before Nausea is offered, so any
remaining Strength and unrelated effects participate in the new Villager's ordinary effect
state; Weakness was removed at initiation. A disconnected starter or one in another dimension
receives neither advancement trigger nor reputation event, but conversion still completes.

The `story/cure_zombie_villager` advancement has parent `story/enter_the_nether`, one unconditioned
`cured_zombie_villager` criterion and a single one-entry requirement, goal frame, Golden Apple
icon and telemetry flag true. Its locked English title is `Zombie Doctor` and description is
`Weaken and then cure a Zombie Villager`. Triggering occurs at completion, not Apple use.

Ordinary Horse, Donkey and Mule feeding:

Unridden Horse and Abstract-Chested-Horse interaction consult live `horse_food` before the shared
hard-coded feed table, so Golden Apple reaches ordinary Horse, Donkey and Mule unless an earlier
adult-tamed secondary-use inventory branch wins. Llama, Camel and Zombie Horse use other food
selectors; an ordinary unmounted Skeleton Horse bypasses the specialized feed-table check.

Golden Apple assigns heal `10`, baby growth `240` seconds and temper increment `10`. On the
server, the table first puts a tamed age-zero equine not already in love into `600`-tick love with
the player as cause. It then, in order:

1. heals `10` when health is below maximum;
2. if age-unlocked and baby, emits the happy-particle offer and ages up `240` seconds;
3. if temper is below maximum and either an earlier effect succeeded or the equine is untamed,
   adds `10` through the clamped temper modifier.

Any success opens the synchronized mouth, plays the subtype eating sound unless silent at volume
`1` and pitch `1+(U1-U2)*0.2`, emits `EAT`, consumes one through the player-ability-aware rule and
returns server success. The Golden Apple's registered player-food/effect transaction is not
invoked.

A tamed full-health adult not already in love therefore consumes solely for love. An untamed
adult never receives love but can consume for healing or temper. A full-health maximum-temper
untamed adult and a full-health already-loving tamed adult return `PASS`. Babies do not enter love
but can heal, grow and raise temper. Horse and Donkey can later use eligible love state to parent;
Mule can retain love but cannot mate.

Vehicle dispatch and equine temptation:

Horse and Abstract-Chested-Horse delegate vehicle interaction to generic `Animal.mobInteract`
before the specialized feed table. Live `horse_food` membership then consumes one for
`600`-tick adult love or advances an age-unlocked baby by
`floor((remainingBabyTicks/20)*0.1)` seconds. It does not heal, alter temper, open the mouth, play
the equine eating sound, emit specialized `EAT`, or apply player-food effects. Already-loving
adults and age-locked babies consume nothing.

A tamed Skeleton Horse vehicle also delegates to the generic path, so an adult can enter unusable
love while unmounted adults and age-locked babies gain no Apple consequence. Zombie Horse, Llama
and Camel selectors reject direct horse-tag membership. Unnatural untamed vehicle fixtures can
receive generic love because `Animal` tests age/love rather than tame state.

Normal Horse, Donkey and Mule goals separately test `horse_tempt_items`. The nearest player within
the live Tempt Range attribute (baseline `10`) holding Golden Apple in either hand can own the
non-line-of-sight goal. It looks at that player and navigates at speed `1.25` until within `2.5`
blocks, cannot be scared by player movement and receives the reduced
`ceil(100/2)=50`-tick cooldown after stopping. Skeleton/Zombie Horse replace this goal set, Llama
uses `llama_tempt_items`, and Camel uses its brain.

Piglin-loved pickup and admiration:

Subject to generic baby-ignore, repellent, attack/admirer, reachability and inventory gates, a
Piglin can want a Golden-Apple item entity. Pickup removes exactly one non-nugget Apple, leaves the
rest in the entity, erases `TIME_TRYING_TO_REACH_ADMIRE_ITEM`, moves the Apple to the off hand and
sets `ADMIRING_ITEM` for `119` ticks. Moving it first drops any previous offhand stack.

When holding ends, an adult generates no barter loot because exact currency remains Gold Ingot.
It first attempts equipment replacement and otherwise stores the Apple stack, throwing overflow
toward a generic random position. Baby finalization retains its separate policy. A player holding
Golden Apple also satisfies `isPlayerHoldingLovedItem`, affecting sensor/look/
nearest-wanted-player and jealous-sound decisions without transfer. Remaining Piglin activity,
memories, navigation, inventory, sound and combat behavior remains `MOB-AI-001`.

Persistence and reload boundary:

Golden-Apple stacks persist and synchronize identity, count and arbitrary ordinary component
patches. They store no active-use progress, hunger, recipe knowledge, chest context, table cursor,
conversion state, animal state or Piglin memory; those belong to their player, world, entity and
machine owners.

A converting Zombie Villager persists `ConversionTime` (or `-1` when inactive), nullable
`ConversionPlayer`, Villager Data/finalized state, optional offers/gossips and XP alongside its
generic entity/effect state. Reload with any conversion time other than sentinel `-1` calls
`startConverting` again using the saved starter and remaining ticks: it restores converting
metadata, removes Weakness, reoffers remaining-duration Strength and rebroadcasts event `16`.
Unload/restart pauses
the timer with no elapsed-time catch-up; future loaded ticks resume the same acceleration logic.

Loot reload changes future unresolved evaluations, including which base or trade-rebalance
replacement record is active. Recipe/advancement reload changes future matching and listeners;
tag reload changes future equine/Piglin admission and temptation. Neither tag nor component reload
changes the exact-identity Zombie-Villager branch. Resource reload independently controls language
and model. Completed eating, crafting, loot, feeding, pickup and conversion transactions are not
replayed.

Client and wire projection:

Generic item-stack encoding projects raw item ID `1014` plus the stack's component patch. Its
common-rarity name uses locked English text `Golden Apple`; the plain class adds no subtype tooltip
or forced glint.

The direct item definition selects generated model `minecraft:item/golden_apple` and the
same-named texture. It appears exactly once in Food & Drinks, ordered Apple, Golden Apple,
Enchanted Golden Apple. Golden Apple also supplies that tab's icon and the Zombie Doctor icon;
neither icon adds another creative entry.

Cure initiation exposes converting metadata, Strength and entity event `16`; completion projects
effect changes, a removed Zombie Villager, newly spawned Villager with a distinct UUID, optional
equipment/state, level event `1027`, advancement and reputation consequences through existing
entity/effect/advancement packet owners. The item adds no new packet layout or numeric mapping.

**Failure behavior:**

starter suppresses credit only. Removed tags or ineligible equine/Piglin state consumes nothing
unless another specified branch succeeds. Missing/replaced data changes only future attempts.
Client-resource absence follows generic fallback and cannot grant authority.

**Boundary cases and quirks:**

The Apple is common and non-glinting despite its exceptional effects. Its recipe unlocks from a
Gold Ingot rather than the center Apple. Igloo table evaluation guarantees one Apple; other
tables use distinct denominators, and trade rebalance changes only Pyramid's Apple odds.
Targeting a non-weak Zombie Villager consumes the interaction rather than starting eating.
Reapplied Weakness permits conversion restart and starter replacement. Acceleration is a
one-percent gated, ordered, 14-special-block scan, not a constant room multiplier. Cure credit is
completion- and same-dimension-dependent. Ordinary equines use love-first hard-coded
`10/240/+10`, while vehicle dispatch switches to percentage growth/love. Piglin admiration does
not imply barter.
