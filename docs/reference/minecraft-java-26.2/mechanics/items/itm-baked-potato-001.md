**Applies when:**

A `baked_potato` stack is cooked from a Potato, generated by an Ice Box, Trial Chambers container
or Trial Spawner, eaten, inserted into a Composter, consumed by a Rabbit-Stew recipe, offered to a
Wandering Trader, moved, renamed, persisted, synchronized, rendered or observed before and after
component, recipe, advancement, loot, trade or resource reload.

**Authoritative state:**

`minecraft:baked_potato` is raw item ID `1259`. It is a common, nondamageable plain `Item` with
maximum stack `64`. Its operational components are:

- food nutrition `5`, saturation `6.0` and omitted/default `can_always_eat=false`;
- the otherwise-default `1.6`-second (`32`-tick) eat consumable, with no consume-effect entries.

The remaining registered components are the common empty modifiers/enchantments/lore, item-break
sound, translated name, direct item-model key, repair cost, swing animation, tooltip display and
use effects. There is no cooldown, use remainder, tool, equipment, repairable, fuel or
identity-specific glint state. It has no direct item-tag membership. Composter admission is
instead code-built by exact item identity at Java float chance `0.85f`.

**Transition and ordering:**

Player consumption:

In-air player use enters the consumable path only when the food listener admits it. Ordinary
survival at food level `20` therefore returns `FAIL`; lower hunger admits use, and an ability that
permits full-hunger eating independently admits it. Block clicks retain block-first handling
before an unconsumed result can reach the edible-item fallback.

Removing only the food component leaves the empty consumable and admits use even at full hunger,
but completion applies no nutrition or saturation. Removing only the consumable makes plain
in-air use pass and prevents food application. Patched food supplies its live nutrition,
saturation and always-edible flag while the consumable retains its empty effect list.

Interruption, release or live-hand/component replacement before completion commits no statistic,
criterion, food change, event or shrink. At successful server completion, generic active-use
ordering emits final eat effects, awards the item-used statistic, triggers `consume_item` against
the live pre-shrink stack, applies food, emits `EAT`, and consumes one unless the user has infinite
materials. Food and saturation are clamped after additions `5` and `6.0`. No consume-effect
probability draw, status-effect offer or remainder occurs.

Baked Potato is one of the `40` independent AND requirements in the telemetry-enabled
`husbandry/balanced_diet` challenge. Its pre-shrink criterion advances before nutrition; only all
40 requirements grant the advancement and its `100` experience, and its icon remains Apple.

Cooking acquisition and recipe progression:

Three exact Potato-input recipes create one default Baked Potato:

| Recipe | Domain | Cooking time | Recipe XP |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `baked_potato` | smelting | omitted/default `200` ticks | `0.35` |
| `baked_potato_from_smoking` | smoking | omitted/default `100` ticks | `0.35` |
| `baked_potato_from_campfire_cooking` | campfire cooking | explicit `600` ticks | data field `0.35` |

All three have category `food`, consume exactly one matching Potato, emit one result and copy no
input component patches. Each no-display `recipes/root` advancement has one OR requirement
containing Potato possession and its own exact recipe unlock; either criterion grants only that
recipe. Obtaining one Potato can therefore grant all three independently. Baked-Potato possession
does not satisfy their inventory criterion.

Furnace and Smoker use the generic furnace-family transaction. Their recipe times are respectively
`200` and `100` processing ticks. Completion records the recipe; a later player extraction runs
the crafted/unlock hooks and awards per accumulated result
`floor(count * 0.35)` experience plus one with probability equal to the fractional part. Hopper
extraction does not perform that award and leaves the accumulated recipe count with the machine.
Fuel choice, tick order, output admission, cancellation, sided access and delayed XP remain
`ITM-FURNACE-001`.

A lit Campfire admits one Potato into the first empty one of four slots, retaining that one input
stack's components and storing total `600`. At the deadline it re-resolves the recipe and emits
one default Baked Potato. Recipe removal instead falls back to the retained Potato, while a
replacement recipe may change the output. Campfire completion ignores the recipe's `0.35` field:
it awards neither recipe XP nor recipe unlock. Extinguishing, independent slot order, retries,
break drops and output motion remain `ITM-CAMPFIRE-001`.

The Potato crop, harvest, village processing, recipe input joins, food-tab placement and furnace
input side remain `BLK-OVERWORLD-CROP-001`; this leaf begins at the exact cooked result and its
specific recipe outputs/progression.

Ancient City and Trial Chambers container acquisition:

Every admitted Baked-Potato entry creates one default stack, replaces its count and permits
repeated selections:

- `chests/ancient_city_ice_box` makes uniform `4..10` rolls over total weight `9`; Baked Potato
  has weight `1`, hence probability `1/9` per roll and uniform count `1..10`, under random sequence
  `minecraft:chests/ancient_city_ice_box`;
- `chests/trial_chambers/intersection_barrel` makes uniform `1..3` rolls over total weight `33`;
  Baked Potato has weight `10`, hence probability `10/33` per roll and uniform count `6..10`,
  under `minecraft:chests/trial_chambers/intersection_barrel`;
- `chests/trial_chambers/supply` makes uniform `3..5` rolls over total weight `18`; Baked Potato
  has weight `2`, hence probability `1/9` per roll and uniform count `2..4`, under
  `minecraft:chests/trial_chambers/supply`.

The Ice-Box alternatives have weights `1/1/2/4` for Suspicious Stew, Golden Carrot, Packed Ice and
Snowball around the potato's weight `1`. The intersection alternatives total the other `23`
weight, and the supply alternatives total the other `16`; their selected functions and all later
rolls still advance the same table cursor. Structure/template placement, lazy container
materialization, repeated output insertion and table-opening criteria remain with the loot,
Ancient City and Trial Chambers owners.

Trial-Spawner acquisition:

All `14` normal Trial-Chamber configurations omit `loot_tables_to_eject` and inherit the default
equal-weight normal consumables/key list. The table choice is fixed once when the encounter's
first registered-player reward becomes due and reused for the remaining registered UUIDs. Normal
consumables therefore wins with probability `1/2`.

`spawners/trial_chamber/consumables` makes one roll over total weight `10`: Cooked Chicken `3`,
Bread `3`, Baked Potato `2`, Regeneration Potion `1` and Swiftness Potion `1`. Conditional on the
fixed table it selects Baked Potato with probability `1/5` and count uniformly `1..3`, using
`minecraft:spawners/trial_chamber/consumables`. The marginal probability for each registered
player evaluation is `1/2 * 1/5 = 1/10`, but multiple players are correlated by the encounter's
shared table choice.

All `14` ominous configurations explicitly fix between the ominous key table at weight `3` and
ominous consumables at weight `7`. Conditional on the `7/10` consumables choice, the one-roll
table has total weight `10`: Cooked Beef `3`, Baked Potato `3`, Golden Carrot `2`, Regeneration
Potion `1` and Strength Potion `1`. Potato probability is `3/10`, count is uniform `2..4`, and
the named sequence is `minecraft:spawners/ominous/trial_chamber/consumables`. Marginal probability
per registered-player evaluation is `21/100`, again correlated across players by the fixed table.

Encounter admission, player cohort, fixed-table persistence, one evaluation per registered UUID,
reward ejection and resets remain `BLK-TRIAL-SPAWNER-001`. A key-table encounter emits no Baked
Potato; a selected consumables table always emits one positive-count alternative.

Rabbit-Stew crafting sink:

No crafting recipe creates Baked Potato. Two shapeless `rabbit_stew` recipes each consume one
Baked Potato, one Cooked Rabbit, one Bowl, one Carrot and respectively one Brown or Red Mushroom,
then emit one default Rabbit Stew. All five inputs are consumed; the Bowl is incorporated into the
filled result rather than returned as a remainder, and arbitrary input component patches do not
propagate.

The two recipe advancements unlock from Cooked-Rabbit possession or their own recipe-unlocked
criterion. Baked-Potato possession does not unlock either. Grid allocation, output admission,
Rabbit Stew's later food/bowl-remainder/Wolf behavior and the complete 22-recipe stew family remain
`ITM-STEW-001`, `ITM-CRAFT-001` and `ITM-ADVANCEMENT-001`.

Wandering-Trader sink:

`wandering_trader/baked_potato_emerald` wants four Baked Potatoes and gives one default Emerald.
It has maximum uses `2`, inherited offer XP `1`, reputation discount coefficient `0.05`, no second
cost, merchant predicate, cost-component predicate, output modifier or double-price enchantment.
The empty component predicate accepts matching Baked-Potato stacks with arbitrary ordinary
component patches; the four paid items and their patches are consumed and never copied to the
Emerald.

The buying trade tag contains exactly Water Bottle, Water Bucket, Milk Bucket, Fermented Spider
Eye, Baked Potato and Hay Bale purchases. Its set requests two distinct records
(`allow_duplicates=false`) with random sequence
`minecraft:trade_set/wandering_trader/buying`. Every record is eligible, so a baseline new trader
includes the Baked-Potato purchase with probability `2/6=1/3`; selected order follows the two
without-replacement draws.

Wandering Trader appends this buying set before its uncommon and common sets. Selection creates an
offer but consumes no Potato; successful generic merchant transactions consume four until the
offer exhausts. Spawning/despawn remains `MOB-WANDERING-TRADER-001`; set selection, price/demand/
reputation adjustment, commit, player reward and menu synchronization remain generic merchant
behavior. This is a Baked-Potato sink, not acquisition.

Composter insertion:

Player-held insertion at level `0` succeeds without RNG. Levels `1..6` consume one
`nextDouble()` and increment exactly when it is strictly below Java float `0.85f`, widened to
`0.8500000238418579` for comparison. Success writes level plus one with flags `3`, emits
`BLOCK_CHANGE`, and `6 -> 7` schedules maturation after `20` ticks; failure preserves state.

Either level-`0..6` result emits level event `1500` with success encoded by whether state changed,
awards the Baked-Potato-used statistic and calls `consume(1, player)`, preserving
infinite-material holders. Level `7` returns success for a held Baked Potato without insertion,
event, statistic or consumption. Level `8` delegates to ordinary item-on-block handling.

Automation exposes one top input slot only below level `7`. It accepts a compostable stack once,
runs the same deterministic-first-level/strict-double transition, emits event `1500`, and removes
the one-slot stack whether chance succeeded or failed. Direct insertion likewise shrinks one after
every admitted level-`0..6` attempt. Maturation `7 -> 8`, ready sound, Bone-Meal extraction and
event `1500` client rendering remain with the Composter and protocol owners.

**Persistence and reload boundary:**

Baked-Potato stacks persist and synchronize identity, count and arbitrary ordinary component
patches. They store no active-use progress, hunger, recipe knowledge, machine slot/timer/fuel/
recipe count, loot cursor, structure or fixed Trial-Spawner context, craft transaction, merchant
offer, Composter level or RNG state. Those belong to player, machine, world, structure, spawner,
menu, merchant and block owners.

Recipe/advancement reload changes future cooking, stew matching and listeners. Loot reload changes
future evaluation of all five tables; Trial-Spawner configuration reload changes future table
fixation without rewriting an already fixed encounter. Trade reload changes future generated
offers without rewriting existing ones. The code-built Composter identity/chance does not come
from a data tag. Completed eating, cooking, loot, crafting, trades and compost attempts are not
replayed. Resource reload independently controls name, model and texture.

**Client and wire projection:**

Generic item-stack encoding projects raw item ID `1259` plus the component patch. Its
common-rarity name uses locked English text `Baked Potato`; the plain class adds no subtype tooltip
or forced glint.

The direct item definition selects generated model `minecraft:item/baked_potato` and its
same-named texture. It appears exactly once and only in Food & Drinks, ordered Potato, Baked
Potato, Poisonous Potato. It has no Ingredients-tab entry and adds no packet layout or numeric
mapping.

**Failure behavior:**

Full-hunger ordinary survival use returns `FAIL`; interruption commits no finish. Invalid,
removed or disabled recipes prevent/fallback as defined by their machine owner. Unselected loot
entries emit their alternatives; key-selected Spawner encounters emit no Potato. Invalid stew
grids produce no result. Missing or exhausted Trader offers commit nothing. Composter failures at
levels `1..6` still consume on admitted attempts, while level `7` consumes nothing. Reloaded or
missing data changes only future evaluation. Client-resource absence follows generic fallback and
cannot grant authority.

**Boundary cases and quirks:**

One Potato unlocks all three cooking recipes, but Campfire completion itself awards neither the
unlock nor the recipe's declared XP. Campfire recipe removal returns the component-bearing input;
successful cooking emits the default result. Normal and ominous Trial rewards share one
encounter-level table choice across players, so their marginal Potato events are correlated.
Baked Potato is consumed into Rabbit Stew, purchased rather than sold by the Wandering Trader,
and composts at level zero without RNG while a failed later admitted attempt still consumes it.
